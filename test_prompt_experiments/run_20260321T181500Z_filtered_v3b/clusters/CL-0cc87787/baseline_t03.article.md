## Body
Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy met with British Prime Minister Keir Starmer in London to discuss ongoing efforts to maintain global focus on Russia’s war in Ukraine. The meeting, which included NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte, centered around signing a new defence pact aimed at enhancing global defensive capabilities against the proliferation of low-cost, high-tech military hardware. Zelenskyy criticized the US for easing sanctions on Russian oil exports, arguing that such actions could bolster Russia’s ability to sustain its military campaign in Ukraine. Meanwhile, diplomatic momentum has slowed due to the escalating tensions in the Middle East, particularly following US-Israeli strikes on Iran. Additionally, Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan offered to host the next round of negotiations between Russia and Ukraine, aiming to address the risks posed by the prolonged war for regional countries and the international order.
