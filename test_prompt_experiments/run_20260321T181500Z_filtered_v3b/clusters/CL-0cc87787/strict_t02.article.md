# Zelenskyy Meets Starmer Amid Rising Tensions

## Summary
Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy met with UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer in London to discuss maintaining sanctions pressure on Russia and signing a new defense pact. The meeting comes amid concerns about global attention shifting towards the Middle East due to the US-Israeli war on Iran.

## Body
Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy met with UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer in London to discuss the ongoing conflict in Ukraine and the need to maintain sanctions pressure on Russia. The meeting took place at 10 Downing Street and included NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte. The discussions centered around peacemaking efforts in Ukraine and the necessity of continuing sanctions against Russia.

The talks come at a time when global attention is shifting towards the Middle East due to the US-Israeli war on Iran. This shift in focus has raised concerns among European leaders about the potential weakening of international resolve against Russia's actions in Ukraine. Zelenskyy criticized the US decision to temporarily ease some sanctions on Russian oil exports, arguing that such moves could embolden Russia and provide it with additional financial resources to sustain its military campaign in Ukraine.

During the meeting, Zelenskyy and Starmer signed a new defense pact aimed at enhancing global defensive capabilities against the proliferation of low-cost, high-tech military hardware. The agreement combines Ukraine's expertise in developing advanced drone interception systems with the UK's industrial capacity for manufacturing and supplying drones and innovative military technologies. Starmer emphasized the strategic importance of emerging military technologies, stating that drones, electronic warfare, and rapid battlefield innovations are now central to national and economic security, particularly in light of the ongoing conflicts in both Ukraine and the Middle East.

Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan offered to host the next round of negotiations between Russia and Ukraine, reflecting the ongoing diplomatic efforts to find a resolution to the conflict. However, these efforts have faced challenges due to the escalation of the war in the Middle East and the shifting priorities of global powers.
