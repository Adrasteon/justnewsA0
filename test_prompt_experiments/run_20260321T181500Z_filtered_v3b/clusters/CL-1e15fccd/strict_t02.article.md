# US Counterterrorism Chief Quits Over Iran War

## Summary
The US counterterrorism chief has resigned, citing that Israel misled President Trump into joining the war against Iran.

## Body
The resignation of the US counterterrorism chief has sparked discussions about the involvement of the United States in the ongoing conflict with Iran. According to reports, the official cited Israel's influence as a primary reason for his departure. This move raises questions about the accuracy of intelligence used by the Trump administration in its decision-making process regarding foreign policy.

Conflicting reports suggest that the situation in the Middle East is complex, with various factions involved in the conflict. The incident at Baghdad’s US embassy, where a fire broke out following reported drone and rocket strikes, underscores the volatile nature of the region. These events highlight the challenges faced by the US in maintaining stability and security in the Middle East while navigating the geopolitical landscape.
