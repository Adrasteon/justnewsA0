# US Counterterrorism Chief Quits Over Iran War

## Summary
The US counterterrorism chief has resigned, citing that Israel misled President Trump into joining the war against Iran.

## Body
The US counterterrorism chief has recently resigned, expressing dissatisfaction with the current administration's approach to the ongoing conflict involving Iran. According to reports, the resignation was prompted by the belief that Israel had misled President Donald Trump into joining the war against Iran. This revelation raises questions about the accuracy of intelligence used by the US government in its decision-making process regarding foreign policy.

The situation is further complicated by conflicting reports and statements from various parties involved. For instance, there are claims that influential figures within the US government might have privately supported the war on Iran, although these claims have been denied by all living former US presidents. Additionally, there are reports of attacks on the US embassy in Baghdad, suggesting that tensions remain high in the region.

These events highlight the complexity and volatility of international relations, particularly concerning the involvement of major powers like the US and Israel in conflicts in the Middle East. The resignation of the counterterrorism chief underscores the potential for misinformation and misdirection in shaping national security policies.
