# Global Response to US Demand for Strait of Hormuz Control

## Summary
The United States has requested international support to secure the Strait of Hormuz, but many countries have declined to participate. France, under President Emmanuel Macron, has explicitly stated that it will not join any operation to open the Strait of Hormuz. Other countries like Australia, Japan, Poland, Spain, Greece, and Sweden have also distanced themselves from military involvement.

## Body
The ongoing conflict between the United States and Iran has escalated to include the Strait of Hormuz, a critical maritime passage for global oil trade. President Donald Trump has made multiple requests to allied nations for assistance in securing the Strait of Hormuz, emphasizing the importance of ensuring free passage for commercial shipping. However, many countries have responded with reluctance or outright refusal to engage in military operations aimed at reopening the strait.

France, under President Emmanuel Macron, has clearly stated that it will not participate in any operations to open or liberate the Strait of Hormuz in the current context. Macron emphasized that France is not a party to the conflict and thus will not engage in such operations. However, he did indicate that once the situation stabilizes, France might consider taking part in an escort system alongside other nations.

Other countries, including Australia, Japan, Poland, Spain, Greece, and Sweden, have also distanced themselves from military involvement in the Strait of Hormuz. These countries have cited various reasons for their reluctance, ranging from diplomatic concerns to the belief that the situation should be resolved through peaceful means rather than military intervention.

President Trump has expressed frustration over the lack of support from allies, particularly those within the NATO alliance. He has criticized countries for not being more enthusiastic about helping the United States, despite the historical protection provided by the US. However, specific details regarding the countries that have shown interest in assisting the US remain unclear.

The refusal of many countries to engage in military operations to secure the Strait of Hormuz highlights the complex geopolitical landscape surrounding the conflict. While the US seeks international support to address the issue, other nations prioritize maintaining neutrality and avoiding direct military engagement in the conflict.
