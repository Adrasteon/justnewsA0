## Body
The U.S. war with Iran has seen significant expenditures, with the Pentagon reporting a cost of approximately $11.3 billion in just one week. However, experts and government officials suggest that the actual costs are much higher, ranging from $1 billion to $2 billion per day. These figures do not account for pre-conflict military buildups and long-term expenses, which could total in the trillions over the next few decades. Critics argue that the funds allocated for the war could instead support domestic programs such as healthcare and education. For instance, the amount spent could provide health coverage for over a million Americans for a year. The uncertainty around the war's duration and costs has led to calls for greater transparency and accountability from the Trump administration.
