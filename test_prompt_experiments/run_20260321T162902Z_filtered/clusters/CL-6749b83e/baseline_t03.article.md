## Body
The upcoming summit between US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping has been delayed due to the ongoing conflict between the US-Israel and Iran. Originally scheduled for March 31 to April 2, the summit has been postponed by up to six weeks. This delay has sparked discussions about potential impacts on US arms sales to Taiwan, which could total US$14 billion. Despite the delay, both sides have maintained communication, with Beijing stating they remain in dialogue with the US regarding the summit. Meanwhile, China continues to engage in various diplomatic activities, including agreeing to live-fire drills with Vietnam and advancing scientific collaborations like rare earth solutions. The delay in the summit is seen by some analysts as a strategic opportunity to ensure more substantive outcomes in stabilizing Sino-American relations.
