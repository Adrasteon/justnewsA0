# Costs of the US War with Iran

## Summary
The US war with Iran has incurred significant costs, with estimates ranging widely. The Pentagon reported spending about $11.3 billion in just one week, while experts and government officials suggest daily costs between $1 billion and $2 billion. Long-term expenses could amount to trillions of dollars over decades.

## Body
The Trump administration is providing fragmented estimates of the costs associated with the U.S. war with Iran, leading to a skewed understanding of the financial impact. The Pentagon initially stated that the U.S. spent approximately $11.3 billion in just one week of the war. However, experts and government officials claim that the actual daily expenditure ranges from $1 billion to $2 billion. These officials also predict that the total cost could rise to a quarter trillion dollars or more within the coming months, with long-term expenses potentially reaching into the trillions of dollars over several decades. Critics argue that the funds allocated to the war could be better utilized for domestic programs such as healthcare and education.
