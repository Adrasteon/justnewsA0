# International Response to Trump's Demand for Strait of Hormuz Control

## Summary
President Donald Trump has requested that NATO allies and other countries send warships to keep the Strait of Hormuz open, which Iran currently controls. Several countries have rejected this demand, including Australia, Japan, Poland, Sweden, and Spain. Other European leaders have also declined to participate.

## Body
President Donald Trump has made a request for NATO allies and other countries to send warships to ensure the Strait of Hormuz remains open. This strategic waterway is currently under control of Iran. Several countries have responded negatively to this request, including Australia, Japan, Poland, Sweden, and Spain. Additionally, European leaders such as Germany and Greece have also ruled out military involvement in the Strait of Hormuz.
