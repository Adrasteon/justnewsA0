## Body
The ongoing conflict between the United States and Israel against Iran has intensified, resulting in significant casualties primarily in Iran. Over 1,500 people have been confirmed dead, with more than 1,400 fatalities occurring within Iran. The United States has sought international support to secure the Strait of Hormuz from Iran's control, particularly from NATO allies and other countries like Australia, Japan, Poland, and Spain. However, many countries have declined to send military ships, citing diplomatic solutions as preferable. President Donald Trump has criticized these countries for their reluctance, emphasizing the historical protection provided by the US. Meanwhile, US casualties have risen, with approximately 200 soldiers wounded, including ten with severe injuries. The number of US deaths stands at 13.
