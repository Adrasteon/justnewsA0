## Body
In the context of the French municipal elections, the Socialist Party and the radical left Front de Gauche (LFI) have set aside their differences to form alliances in key cities ahead of the runoff votes scheduled for Sunday. This move comes despite previous tensions, including the Socialists' condemnation of LFI leader Jean-Luc Mélenchon's 'intolerable antisemitic remarks.' Meanwhile, the ongoing conflict in the Middle East continues to impact various regions. French President Emmanuel Macron has stated that France will not participate in operations to open or liberate the Strait of Hormuz until the situation has calmed down. He emphasized that France is not a party to the conflict and will not engage in such operations. Additionally, a senior U.S. counterterrorism official has resigned in protest against the war with Iran, citing that Iran did not pose an imminent threat to the United States and that the war was initiated due to pressure from Israel and its influential lobby groups. The conflict has also affected the fishing industry in the United Arab Emirates, where fishermen have been confined to ports along the coast near the Strait of Hormuz, leading to a standstill in the sector and soaring fish market prices.
