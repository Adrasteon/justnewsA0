# Zelenskyy Meets Starmer Amid Rising Tensions

## Summary
Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy met with UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer in London to discuss maintaining sanctions pressure on Russia and signing a new defence pact. The meeting comes as global attention shifts towards the Middle East due to the US-Israeli war on Iran.

## Body
During the meeting, Zelenskyy and Starmer discussed the need to maintain sanctions pressure on Russia and signed a new defence pact aimed at enhancing global defensive capabilities against low-cost, high-tech military hardware. NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte also joined the meeting to discuss peacemaking efforts in Ukraine. The discussions were held against the backdrop of the US partially rolling back sanctions against Russia to stabilize oil prices disrupted by the Middle East conflict.
