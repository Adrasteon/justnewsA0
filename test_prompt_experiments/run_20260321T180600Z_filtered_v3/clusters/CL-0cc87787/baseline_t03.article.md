## Body
Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy met with British Prime Minister Keir Starmer in London to discuss the ongoing conflict in Ukraine and the impact of recent geopolitical events. The meeting, which included NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte, focused on maintaining sanctions pressure on Russia and enhancing global defensive capabilities against low-cost, high-tech military hardware. Zelenskyy expressed concern over the US decision to ease sanctions on Russian oil exports, arguing that such moves could bolster Russia's ability to sustain its military campaign in Ukraine. Meanwhile, European leaders, including French President Emmanuel Macron, are emphasizing the need to maintain pressure on Russia despite the shifting focus towards the Middle East due to the US-Israeli war on Iran. Additionally, Turkey offered to host the next round of negotiations between Russia and Ukraine, aiming to address the risks posed by the prolonged war for regional stability.
