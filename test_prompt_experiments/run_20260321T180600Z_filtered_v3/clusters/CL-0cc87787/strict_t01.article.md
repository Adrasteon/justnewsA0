# Zelenskyy Meets Starmer Amid Rising Tensions

## Summary
Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy met with British Prime Minister Keir Starmer in London to discuss maintaining sanctions pressure on Russia and signing a new defence pact. The meeting comes amid rising tensions in the Middle East due to the US-Israeli war on Iran.

## Body
During the meeting, discussions centered around peacemaking efforts in Ukraine and the need to maintain sanctions pressure on Russia. Zelenskyy criticized the US for easing sanctions on Russian oil exports, arguing that this could strengthen Russia's position in Ukraine. Starmer emphasized the importance of focusing on Ukraine amidst the unfolding conflict in the Middle East.
