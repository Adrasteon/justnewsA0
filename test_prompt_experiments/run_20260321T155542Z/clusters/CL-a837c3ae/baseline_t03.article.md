## Body
France Médias Monde and its partners utilize cookies and similar technologies to collect non-sensitive information such as IP addresses from users. This data is processed to enhance user experience, monitor audience engagement, provide social network functionalities, and display targeted advertisements. Users have the option to accept, customize, or deny consent through the 'Manage my privacy settings' link in the menu. Additionally, France Médias Monde collects personal data from subscribers to deliver tailored content and monitor audience preferences. Subscribers can manage their privacy settings, unsubscribe from newsletters, and exercise their rights under the French Data Protection Act and the European Regulation on the protection of personal data. The company also provides detailed information on how it uses personal data and outlines the rights of individuals regarding their data.
