# Tennessee Teens Sue Elon Musk's xAI Over AI-Generated Child Sexual Abuse Material

## Summary
Three Tennessee teenagers have filed a class-action lawsuit against Elon Musk's artificial intelligence company, xAI, alleging that its technology was used to generate nonconsensual nude and sexually explicit images and videos of them when they were minors.

## Body
The lawsuit claims that an unnamed app, which uses xAI's algorithm, was used to create these images. The plaintiffs accuse xAI of deliberately licensing its technology to app makers, often outside the U.S., to potentially outsource liability for their tool. The complaint highlights that the perpetrator had a close relationship with one of the plaintiffs and used personal photos to generate the images and videos. The plaintiffs seek damages for emotional distress and other harms caused by the images.
