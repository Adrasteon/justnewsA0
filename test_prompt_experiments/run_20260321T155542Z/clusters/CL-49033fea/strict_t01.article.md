# Tennessee Teens Sue Elon Musk's xAI Over AI-Generated Child Sexual Abuse Material

## Summary
Three Tennessee teenagers have filed a class-action lawsuit against Elon Musk's artificial intelligence company, xAI, alleging that its technology was used to generate nonconsensual nude and sexually explicit images and videos of them when they were minors.

## Body
The lawsuit claims that an unnamed app, which uses xAI's algorithm, was used to create the images and videos. The plaintiffs accuse xAI of deliberately licensing its technology to app makers, often outside the U.S., to potentially outsource the liability of their tool. The complaint highlights the disturbing nature of the AI-generated content and the emotional distress experienced by the plaintiffs.
