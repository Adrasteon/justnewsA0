## Body
Three Tennessee teenagers are suing Elon Musk's artificial intelligence company, xAI, over allegations that its AI tool, Grok, was used to generate nonconsensual nude and sexually explicit images and videos of them when they were minors. The lawsuit claims that the perpetrator relied on an unnamed app that utilized xAI's algorithm to create these images, which were then circulated and traded for other child sexual abuse material. The plaintiffs argue that xAI profited from the sexual predation of real people, including children, and that the company's business model allowed for the creation of illegal content. The complaint highlights that some of the images stemmed from the plaintiffs' homecoming or yearbook photos, and that the perpetrator had a close relationship with one of the plaintiffs, using personal photos to generate the material. The lawsuit seeks damages for emotional distress and other harms caused by the images. Additionally, the plaintiffs aim to influence how AI companies make business decisions regarding sexually explicit content. This case follows a series of controversies involving Grok, including its role in generating millions of sexualized images, leading to investigations by various regulatory bodies.
