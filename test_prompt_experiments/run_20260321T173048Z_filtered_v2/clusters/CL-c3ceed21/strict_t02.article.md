# Joe Kent Resigns as Director of National Counterterrorism Center

## Summary
Joe Kent, the director of the National Counterterrorism Center, resigned from his position due to disagreements with President Donald Trump's handling of the U.S.-Israel war with Iran. Kent stated that Iran posed no imminent threat to the United States and criticized the war as being influenced by Israel and its supporters.

## Body
Joe Kent, a former Army Special Forces officer and political candidate, resigned from his role as the director of the National Counterterrorism Center. Kent cited his concerns over the ongoing war in Iran, stating that Iran did not pose an imminent threat to the United States and that the war was initiated due to pressure from Israel and its supporters. Kent's resignation highlights internal disagreements within the Trump administration regarding the war in Iran.
