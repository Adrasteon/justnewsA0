## Body
Joe Kent, the director of the National Counterterrorism Center, resigned on Tuesday, citing his opposition to the ongoing war in Iran. Kent, a former Army Special Forces officer and a Trump loyalist, argued that Iran posed no imminent threat to the United States and that the war was driven by pressure from Israel and its supporters in the U.S. media. Kent emphasized that he could not support sending young Americans to fight in a war that he deemed unjustified. In his resignation letter, Kent expressed his disappointment with the current administration's foreign policy direction, particularly the decision to engage militarily with Iran. He criticized the misinformation campaign orchestrated by high-ranking Israeli officials and influential American media figures, claiming that these efforts undermined President Trump’s “America First” platform and sowed pro-war sentiments. Kent’s resignation marks a significant departure from a man long considered among Trump’s most committed loyalists. However, President Trump responded by stating that he always thought Kent was “weak on security” and that Kent’s resignation was a positive development.
