## Body
The ongoing conflict between the United States and Israel against Iran has intensified, with strikes causing significant casualties primarily in Iran. Over 1,500 people have been confirmed dead, with more than 1,400 fatalities occurring in Iran. The number of US soldiers wounded in the conflict has risen to approximately 200, with most suffering minor injuries and about 180 having returned to duty. Ten soldiers have sustained severe injuries, and thirteen have been killed. Meanwhile, President Donald Trump has sought international support to secure the Strait of Hormuz from Iran's control, emphasizing the strategic importance of the waterway. However, many countries, including Australia, Japan, Poland, Spain, Greece, and Sweden, have rejected his demands, citing diplomatic concerns and reluctance to engage in military operations. European leaders, such as Germany's Defense Minister Boris Pistorius and Italy's Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani, have also expressed opposition to military intervention, advocating instead for diplomatic solutions.
