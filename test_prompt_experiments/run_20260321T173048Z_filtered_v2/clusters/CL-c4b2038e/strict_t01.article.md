# International Response to Trump's Demand for Strait of Hormuz Control

## Summary
President Donald Trump has requested that NATO allies and other countries send warships to keep the Strait of Hormuz open, amid tensions with Iran. Several countries have rejected or distanced themselves from the request, including Australia, Japan, Poland, Spain, Greece, and Sweden. French President Emmanuel Macron stated that France will not participate in operations to open the Strait of Hormuz but is willing to contribute to an escort system once the situation stabilizes.
