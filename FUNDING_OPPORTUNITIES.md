# Funding Opportunities for JustNews System

## 1. Tarbell Center for AI Journalism Grants
- **Amount**: $1,000 - $20,000
- **Focus**: Original reporting on AI and its impacts
- **Relevance**: Perfect match for JustNews' AI-driven news analysis capabilities
- **Links**:
  - Main grants page: https://www.tarbellcenter.org/grants
  - Specific 2026 grants info: https://grantsdatabase.org/2026/01/30/tarbell-grants-2026-original-reporting-on-ai-and-its-impacts/

## 2. JournalismAI Innovation Challenge (Google News Initiative)
- **Amount**: $50,000 or $250,000 grants
- **Focus**: Experimentation and implementation of AI technologies in news organizations
- **Relevance**: Directly supports AI innovation in journalism like JustNews provides
- **Links**:
  - Main page: https://www.journalismai.info/
  - Announcement of 2025 grants: https://www.journalismai.info/blog/journalismai-awards-35-ai-innovation-grants
  - EU Funding version: https://eufundingportal.eu/ai-innovation-challenge-for-news-publishers/

## 3. Mozilla Foundation Democracy x AI Cohort
- **Amount**: $50,000 grants (with potential $250,000 Sustain Track)
- **Focus**: Technology at intersection of AI and democratic governance
- **Relevance**: JustNews' news aggregation and analysis supports democratic accountability
- **Links**:
  - Main announcement: https://grantedai.com/blog/mozilla-democracy-ai-cohort-50k-grants-2026

## 4. Lenfest Institute/OpenAI/Microsoft AI Collaborative
- **Amount**: Grant funding + enterprise cloud credits
- **Focus**: AI experimentation for news business sustainability
- **Relevance**: Provides both funding and technical infrastructure support
- **Links**:
  - Announcement: https://news.microsoft.com/2024/10/22/lenfest-institute-openai-and-microsoft-announce-10-million-ai-collaborative-and-fellowship-program-for-us-metro-news-organizations/
  - Program details: https://www.lenfestinstitute.org/our-work/lenfest-ai-collaborative-and-fellowship-program/

## 5. Global AI Accelerator for Media (IPI)
- **Amount**: Up to $14,000 funding + training
- **Focus**: AI adoption for independent media in Global Majority countries
- **Relevance**: Supports smaller outlets using AI like JustNews enables
- **Links**:
  - Program page: https://ipi.media/innovation/global-ai-accelerator-for-media/

## 6. Open Society's Applied AI in Journalism Challenge
- **Amount**: $5,000 - $25,000 grants
- **Focus**: Prototype AI applications in mission-driven newsrooms
- **Relevance**: Early-stage funding for AI journalism tools
- **Links**:
  - Report/publication: https://www.opensocietyfoundations.org/publications/open-society-s-applied-ai-in-journalism-challenge

## 7. Cloud Infrastructure Credits
- **Amount**: Typically $50,000-$100,000 in credits
- **Focus**: Computational resources for AI/ML workloads
- **Relevance**: JustNews' crawler/synthesizer could benefit from cloud compute credits
- **Links**:
  - Azure Cloud Credit Grants (via Stanford HAI): https://hai.stanford.edu/research/grant-programs/azure-cloud-credit-grants
  - General cloud program info: https://funding.partnerplex.ai/

## NEW: FACT-CHECKING & MISINFORMATION FUNDING OPPORTUNITIES
*(Based on JustNews' core mission of providing fact-checked, neutral news)*

### 8. International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN) Global Fact Check Fund
- **Amount**: Various grant types (GROW: up to $50,000, ENGAGE: up to $100,000)
- **Focus**: Supporting fact-checking organizations and combating misinformation
- **Relevance**: Direct alignment with JustNews' fact-checking and misinformation removal objectives
- **Links**:
  - Main fund page: https://www.poynter.org/ifcn-grants-funding/
  - Recent awards announcement: https://www.poynter.org/ifcn/2025/global-fact-check-fund-awards-2-million-to-20-fact-checking-groups-across-15-countries/
  - GROW grants info: https://www.poynter.org/ifcn/2024/ifcn-awards-grow-2024-grants-to-20-projects-from-12m-global-fund/
  - ENGAGE grants info: https://www.poynter.org/ifcn/2025/ifcn-awards-engage-grants-global-fact-check-fund/

### 9. MacArthur Foundation Journalism & Media Grants
- **Amount**: Varies (examples show $350,000 grants)
- **Focus**: Fighting disinformation and supporting journalistic integrity
- **Relevance**: Direct support for tools that combat misinformation like JustNews
- **Links**:
  - Example award: https://rjionline.org/news/verdad-earns-350000-macarthur-grant-to-boost-fight-against-disinformation/
  - Foundation journalism page: https://www.macfound.org/programs/journalism-media/

### 10. Poynter Institute Fact-Checking Grants
- **Amount**: Various amounts through IFCN partnerships
- **Focus**: Sustainability and growth for fact-checking organizations
- **Relevance**: Supports the infrastructure needed for ongoing fact-checking operations
- **Links**:
  - Global Fact Check Fund: https://jx-fund.org/?jie_posts=global-fact-check-fund
  - Poynter grants overview: https://www.poynter.org/grants-fellowships/

## Strategic Recommendations:
1. **Tarbell Grants** and **JournalismAI/Lenfest programs** for AI innovation in journalism
2. **Cloud credit programs** to reduce operational costs for crawler/synthesizer workloads
3. **IFCN Global Fact Check Fund** and **MacArthur Foundation grants** for fact-checking focus
4. **Mozilla/Open Society funds** for accountability/democracy-focused applications

JustNews' combination of BBC crawling, web search for related articles, synthesis capabilities, and explicit focus on removing misinformation, fact-checking, and providing neutral coverage positions it exceptionally well for both journalism technology funding AND fact-checking specific funding opportunities. The system addresses key interests across multiple funder domains: AI innovation in news, democratic accountability, technological infrastructure for media, and direct misinformation/fact-checking initiatives.
