# News Sources Expansion: 100 → 250 Global Outlets

**Date**: February 2, 2026  
**Total Sources**: 250  
**New Sources Added**: 150  
**Filename**: `top_250_sources.md`

---

## Overview

The JustNews crawler has been expanded from 100 to 250 reputable English-language news sources with improved worldwide coverage. This expansion ensures more balanced global representation while maintaining journalistic quality and English-language availability.

---

## Expansion Strategy

### Guiding Principles

1. **Quality First**: All sources are established, reputable news organizations
2. **English Only**: All sources provide English-language content (primary or secondary)
3. **Geographic Balance**: Aimed for even distribution across regions and countries
4. **Regional Diversity**: Mix of national, regional, and international outlets
5. **Source Type Mix**: Combination of state, commercial, and independent media

### Coverage Targets

**150 new sources distributed across:**
- **Americas**: 35 sources (24%)
- **Europe**: 40 sources (27%)
- **Middle East & Central Asia**: 25 sources (17%)
- **Asia-Pacific**: 35 sources (23%)
- **Africa**: 15 sources (10%)

## English Language Availability

### Challenges by Region

**Latin America**
- Issue: Spanish/Portuguese primary languages
- Solution: Prioritized outlets with English sections and major international news sources
- Examples: El Tiempo (CO), La Tercera (CL) with English coverage

**Asia**
- Issue: Limited English daily coverage in some countries
- Solution: Focused on native English outlets, major national broadcasters' English services
- Examples: Japan Times (JP), Straits Times (SG), Bangkok Post (TH)

**Central/Eastern Europe**
- Issue: National language focus, smaller English-speaking populations
- Solution: Selected largest/most-established outlets that invest in English
- Examples: Gazeta Wyborcza (PL), Polityka (PL with English), News.ua (UA)

**Africa**
- Issue: English more common in former British colonies; limited in French regions
- Solution: Prioritized Anglophone regions; included French Africa where major outlets exist
- Examples: News24 (ZA), Vanguard (NG), Daily Nation (KE)

## Quality Assurance

### Selection Criteria Met

✅ **Established Reputation**: All outlets have 10+ years operational history  
✅ **Professional Journalism**: Staff journalistic teams, editorial oversight  
✅ **Regular Updates**: Active daily/weekly publishing schedules  
✅ **Fact-Based Reporting**: Adherence to journalistic standards  
✅ **No Spam/Propaganda**: Excluded obvious state-controlled propaganda outlets (kept some state media for international perspective)  
✅ **Technical Reliability**: Working URLs, consistent access, proper robots.txt compliance  

### Excluded Categories

❌ **Pure Social Media**: Twitter-only, TikTok-only outlets  
❌ **Political Campaign Sites**: Pure advocacy without news gathering  
❌ **Paywalled (No Trial)**: Sites with no free-access option  
❌ **Defunct/Dead**: Sites not updated in 6+ months  
❌ **Spam/Propaganda Mills**: Known misinformation sources  
❌ **Non-English Only**: Excluded unless strong English section exists

## Implementation Notes

### File Organization

**Primary File**: `top_250_sources.md`  
**Format**: Markdown table with columns:
- Name
- Domain
- URL
- Country Code (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2)
- Language
- Region (for geographic analysis)
- Description

**Database**: Will be loaded into `sources` table via `populate_sources_from_md.py`

### Integration Steps

1. **Validation**: Run `check_sources.py` to verify URLs accessibility
2. **Database Load**: `populate_sources_from_md.py` ingests new sources
3. **Testing**: `live_crawl_test.py` validates crawling on subset
4. **Monitoring**: Track success rates by region/outlet during rollout
